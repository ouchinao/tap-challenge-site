# Tap Challenge Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Tap Challenge (the "App") handles information about you.

Effective date: November 23, 2026

## 1. Information the App stores

The App does not send any information to the developer's servers. The following is stored only on your device:

- Personal bests, today's bests, total taps, play streak and number of plays for each mode
- Your sound, vibration and skin settings
- Whether you purchased Remove Ads
- When and how often ads were shown (to keep ads spaced out)

This information is deleted when you delete the App.

## 2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC.
To serve ads, Google may collect information from your device, such as the advertising identifier, IP address, device model, OS version and records of ad views and interactions.

- The App may ask for permission to track you on iOS. If you don't allow it, ads are still shown, but your device's advertising identifier is not used.
- Users in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent about how their information is used for ads.
- Users in some US states can opt out of the sale or sharing of their personal information.
- You can change these choices at any time in the App under Settings > Privacy settings.

For how Google handles information, see Google's Privacy Policy (<https://policies.google.com/privacy>) and
How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services (<https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>).

## 3. Leaderboards (Game Center)

The App offers leaderboards through Apple's Game Center. Scores you submit and your nickname are shown to other players through Game Center.
Information in Game Center is handled under Apple's Privacy Policy.

## 4. In-app purchases

Remove Ads is purchased through Apple's App Store. The App never receives your payment information.

## 5. Age checks

For users in regions where Apple determines age assurance applies, the App may use Apple's Declared Age Range to learn your age range (under 13, 13 to 17, or 18 and over).
The age range is used only to choose ad settings (such as child-directed treatment or non-personalized ads) and is never stored on your device or on any server.

## 6. Children

The App is intended for users aged 13 and over. For users known to be under 13, the App does not ask for tracking permission and shows ads with child-directed treatment.

## 7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will announce it on the App's store page and on this page.
