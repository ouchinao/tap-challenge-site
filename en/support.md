# Tap Challenge Support

Tap Challenge is a tapping game for iPhone that measures how many times you can tap in 10 seconds. It is developed by an individual developer, ouchinao.

## Contact

Please send bug reports, questions, and requests through the [contact form (Google Forms)](https://forms.gle/NtesGYzg8g6DDNUe6).

For bug reports, the following helps us look into the problem:

- Your device model and iOS version
- Which mode or screen you were on, what you did, and what happened

As this app is developed by one person, replies may take a few days.

## FAQ

### How do I remove ads?

Buy "Remove ads" from the top right of the home screen or from Settings (one-time purchase). Banner and full-screen ads stop, and your taps always count double toward your total.

### I bought it, but ads still appear / Ads came back on my new phone

Tap "Restore purchases" in Settings. If you use the same Apple Account you bought it with, ad removal comes back.

### My score doesn't appear on the leaderboards

The leaderboards use Game Center. Sign in under "Game Center" in the iPhone Settings app. Rounds judged as machine-like tapping (shown as an "Unofficial" record on the result screen) are not sent to the leaderboards.

### Can I move my records to another device?

Records are stored only on your device and are not sent to the developer's servers. They cannot be moved to another device, except by restoring the device from a backup.

### How do I turn off sounds or vibration?

Switch "Sound effects" and "Vibration" in Settings. Sound effects also stay silent while your iPhone is in Silent mode.

### Does the app send personal information?

The app does not send information to the developer's servers. For how ads (Google AdMob) and leaderboards (Game Center) handle information, see the [Privacy Policy](privacy).
